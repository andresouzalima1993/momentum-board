Strategy rules

  • Donchian 55 (daily). Entry on a 55-day channel breakout, exit on the opposite 27-day channel, 3-ATR trailing Stop-loss at the daily close. Position size targets 35% annual portfolio volatility, at most 3× gross Leverage and 25% of the sleeve per coin. Universe: top 40 perps by 30-day dollar volume.
  • HBO ensemble (hourly). Universe: top 20 perps by 30-day dollar volume, ranked point-in-time at the previous daily close. Orders are filled when the hourly bar closes, at the live price of the next run. Risk 0.5% of sub-account per trade, max 8 positions and 3× gross per sub-account, 3% daily loss limit.
  • Execution realism. Fills use the live mid plus real order-book Slippage (HBO: the larger of the real book and a 3/4/6 bp assumption by liquidity rank). Donchian assumes 70% maker fills at 0.015% and 30% taker at 0.045%; HBO is all taker at 0.045%. Sizes respect each coin's size decimals, the US$ 10 minimum order and per-coin maximum Leverage (initial margin ≤ 80% of equity). Stale data stops the run.
  • Funding. Real hourly Hyperliquid funding is charged on every open position (shorts receive when the rate is positive).
  • Rebalance. Once a month the two sleeves are reset to 50/50 by moving virtual cash (no trading cost).
Paper simulation for research only, not investment advice. Data: Hyperliquid public info API (candles, mids, order books and funding history). Times in BRT (America/Sao_Paulo); daily candles close at 00:00 UTC = 21:00 BRT. Past and simulated results do not guarantee future results.
