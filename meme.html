Meme desk: on-chain memecoins, four paper books

A desk of coded "seats" scans new Solana pools every 5 minutes (SEARCH), checks contract and rug risk (RISK, WHALE), measures order flow and pool depth (FLOW, LIQUIDITY), and a JUDGE answers four closed questions with a fixed buy gate. The same buy signals feed four separate paper books: MOMO enters right after the verdict, DIP waits for a 15% pullback; CONS starts with US$ 1,000 and risks 1.5% per trade, LADDER starts with US$ 100, bets big while small and de-risks as it grows. Goal of LADDER: a high multiple on a small bank, accepting a real chance of losing it.

Books

Multiple = (trading bank + locked profits) ÷ start. Unrealized P&L is at liquidation value (after price impact, DEX fee and network fee). Drawdown is peak-to-trough of the total bank.

Growth, ruin and sample size

How likely each book is to multiply or go to zero, from a bootstrap of its own closed trades through its sizing rules (100 trades). Median vs mean shows how much a few big winners carry the average. Under 30 trades the numbers are noise.

De-risking ladder

LADDER books: 30% of the bank per entry up to 3x, tapering to 6% by 10x, then the conservative rules. At each doubling 33% of the new gains are locked and never traded.

Open positions

Exits run on 1-minute bars: stop first if a bar hits both, gap-through fills, 1% slippage plus pool impact; liquidity gone = −100%; a top holder selling >8% of supply in ~5 min triggers a whale exit.
