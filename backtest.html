What the audit checked

  • Universe. Both legs rank coins point-in-time by trailing 30-day volume at the previous close. HBO covers 97.6% of top-20 coin-days (the rest have no proxy history); re-running with the strict top 20 of all Hyperliquid perps gave a similar result. Coins fully removed from the API are missing from the Donchian history (small residual survivorship).
  • Look-ahead. Signals were re-computed on truncated histories and matched exactly: no future data is used. Orders act on the next bar.
  • Costs and funding. Both legs include real hourly funding. Doubling costs, doubling funding and delaying HBO fills are in the table above.
  • Combo. The earlier figure assumed free daily rebalancing and a hypothetical 4% yield on idle cash for the Donchian leg only. Both were removed in the base case.
  • Live engine. The HBO paper engine replays the backtest bar by bar with identical results (difference below US$ 0.000001 over 4,272 hourly bars on Hyperliquid candles).
Paper simulation for research only, not investment advice. Data: Hyperliquid public info API (candles, mids, order books and funding history). Times in BRT (America/Sao_Paulo); daily candles close at 00:00 UTC = 21:00 BRT. Past and simulated results do not guarantee future results.
