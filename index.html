Strategy rules

The same rules as the backtest, in plain language. Decisions are made once a day, at the daily close.

  • Universe. Each day, the 40 Hyperliquid perpetuals with the highest average daily dollar volume over the last 30 days.
  • Long entry. Buy when a coin closes above its highest close of the previous 55 days (breakout).
  • Short entry. Sell short when a coin closes below its lowest close of the previous 55 days (breakdown).
  • Exit channel. Close a Long when the price closes below the lowest close of the previous 27 days; close a Short when it closes above the highest close of the previous 27 days.
  • Stop-loss. A trailing stop 3 × ATR(14) away from the best close since entry. If a close crosses it, the position is closed and is only re-entered after a new signal.
  • Position sizing. More volatile coins get smaller positions (inverse volatility, 20% volatility floor). The whole book targets 35% annual volatility, with at most 25% of equity per coin and total leverage of at most 3×.
  • Rebalancing. A position is only resized when it drifts more than 10% from its target, to save fees.
  • Leaving the universe. If a coin drops out of the top 40 by volume, its position is closed.
  • Costs. Maker-order model: 70% of each fill at a 0.015% maker fee and no slippage; the remaining 30% at a 0.045% taker fee plus 0.03% slippage (0.033% blended cost per side). Real hourly funding of each coin, summed per day.
  • Idle cash yield. Unlevered cash (max(0, 1 − gross exposure) at the previous close) earns 4% per year, accrued each day and shown as its own P&L line. This is a conservative stablecoin / T-bill-style assumption, not a Hyperliquid payment.
  • Execution. Fills at the daily closing price, 00:00 UTC (21:00 BRT). The stop-loss is checked only at that close, not intraday.
  • Safety. Paper only: public market data, no orders, no wallets, no keys. Missed days are processed one by one on the next run.
